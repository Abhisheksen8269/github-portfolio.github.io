Academic & Engineering Profile

About My Work

Bridging mathematical safety guarantees with physical, embedded hardware deployment in real-world robotics.

I specialize in autonomous navigation, state estimation, and provably safe non-linear control for ground and aerial robots. My research bridges non-parametric Bayesian learning (Gaussian Processes) with formal non-linear control theory (Control Barrier Functions) to guarantee safe trajectory navigation under dynamic uncertainty — deployed on physical Crazyflie 2.1 nano-quadrotors and industrial outdoor UGVs.

Unlike purely simulation-based algorithms, my work emphasizes closed-loop physical deployment. I have formulated mathematical proofs for probabilistic forward invariance, benchmarked algorithms across hundreds of simulation runs, and physically flown them on nano-quadrotors and heavy payload rovers using ROS 2, PX4 Autopilot, Livox 4D LiDAR, and Jetson companion computers.

Undergraduate Capstone Thesis

Probabilistic Safe Navigation in Obstacle-Cluttered Dynamic Environments: A Gaussian Process–Enabled Control Barrier Function Approach

Unified Framework: GP-CBF-QP Formal Safety Proof Crazyflie 2.1 Nano-UAV UWB Loco Positioning + IMU

GP-CBF-QP: Safe Autonomous Quadrotor Navigation

Supervised by: Dr. Selvajyothi K (Associate Prof., IIITDM Kancheepuram) & Dr. Satadal Ghosh (Assistant Prof., Aerospace Engineering, IIT Madras)

Navigating autonomous aerial vehicles through dynamic environments with moving obstacles of unknown velocities poses fundamental safety risks. This research derived GP-CBF-QP, uniting non-parametric Bayesian regression with control theory. A Gaussian Process with squared exponential kernels infers unknown velocity vector fields and quantifies epistemic uncertainty online, systematically embedding these bounds into Control Barrier Functions (CBFs) via concentration inequalities. A Quadratic Program (QP) minimally adjusts nominal control inputs to ensure probabilistic forward invariance with formal mathematical proof: P[h(x(t), x_o(t)) ≥ 0, ∀t ≥ 0] ≥ 1 - δ.
